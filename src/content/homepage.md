whoWeAreText: >-
  Hlokomela was established in 2005 in response to research that highlighted
  a critical need for health care among farm workers and other vulnerable
  populations in the greater Hoedspruit area. Since then, Hlokomela has been
  a prominent and respected role player in the health and well-being of the
  local community, serving about 30 000 people annually (direct and indirect
  beneficiaries).
whoWeBecameText: >-
  In 2025 along with hundreds of other NPOs in South Africa, we faced a
  funding crisis that would redefine the way we work, plan and survive to
  carry on serving communities. Despite this, Hlokomela celebrated a major
  achievement when the organisation had its 20th anniversary on 20 June
  2025. We expanded on health care services available to clients with the
  addition of on-site chiropractic and primary eye care services.
